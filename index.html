Questions before you start

How long does a project take?

A logo usually takes about a week. A full brand identity takes two to three weeks. Brand and website together takes three to eight weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly you send feedback.

How do payments work?

You pay a 50% deposit to start and the balance before I send the final files. Payment is by bank transfer.

What if I don't like the first designs?

Every package includes revision rounds. I also ask questions up front about your business and customers, so the first round is already close to what you want.

Will I own my logo and files?

Yes. Once you choose to pay for it, the final logo and brand files belong to you.

Do you build the website too?

Yes. I design and code your site, connect enquiries, and put it live. Domain and hosting fees are paid by you directly to the provider, and I can help you with that too.

Can you work with me if I am not in Nigeria?

Yes. Everything can be remotely, through calls, so you can be anywhere.
